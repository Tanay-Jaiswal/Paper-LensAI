# PaperLens report: Neurocache: Efficient Vector Retrieval for Long-range Language Modeling

*14 pages · 2 claims · reproducibility 58.7/100 (moderate)*

## Summary

- Our experiments show the effectiveness of Neurocache both for models trained from scratch and for pre-trained models such as Llama2- 7B and Mistral-7B when enhanced with the cache mechanism. *(p.1)*
- Notably, Neurocache enhances the maximum context length of models like Llama2-7B and Mistral-7B to 128K tokens. *(p.9)*

**Problem**
- Neurocache improves upon previous methods by (1) storing compressed states, which reduces cache size; (2) performing a single retrieval operation per token which increases inference speed; and (3) extending the retrieval window to neighboring states, which improves both language modeling and downstream task accuracy. *(p.1)*
- However, these approaches often struggle to utilize their extended contexts (Liu et al., 2023) fully. *(p.1)*

**Approach**
- In response to these challenges, we introduce Neurocache. *(p.1)*
- This training, using a causal language modeling objective on a corpus of long documents, enables the models to efficiently utilize the Neurocache system. *(p.4)*

**Results**
- We also compare Neurocache with text retrieval methods and show improvements in single-document questionanswering and few-shot learning tasks. *(p.1)*
- Neurocache advances over exiting methods by reducing the cache size through the storage of compressed states, performing a single retrieval operation per token to boost inference speed, and extending the retrieval window to include neighboring states for improved language modeling and downstream accuracy. *(p.2)*
- For instance, in HotpotQA, Text Retrieval with the Mistral-7B model achieves the highest F1 score of 40.92. *(p.8)*

**Limitations**
- A notable limitation is Neurocache’s performance in multi-document scenarios, suggesting potential challenges in contexts that require integration of information from multiple sources. *(p.9)*
- This limitation suggests that our current findings may not fully capture the model’s adaptability and efficiency in diverse application scenarios. *(p.9)*
