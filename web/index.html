What the audit produces for each paper

  1. Summary

    Sentences quoted verbatim from the paper, each with its page.

  2. Evidence Ledger

    Each headline claim linked to the passages in the paper body that back it, or marked "no supporting evidence found".

  3. Reproducibility

    A 15-item reporting checklist with the quote that earned each credit and the items that are missing; 10 more items for Computer Science papers (code link, hardware, compute, seeds in tables, baselines ...).

  4. Evidence gaps

    Rule-based flags for a reviewer, such as a figure in the abstract that never appears in the results.

  5. Numbers check

    The paper's own arithmetic redone: percentages of counts, stated differences, table totals and averages, F1, confidence intervals and p-values.

  6. Revision checklist

    Every finding turned into a concrete fix for the authors: what to change, why, and on which page.
