PaperLens AI

Research evidence & reproducibility auditor

Two generated example papers (not real studies), analysed live.

What you get for each paper

Summary

Sentences quoted verbatim from the paper, each with its page.

Evidence Ledger

Each headline claim linked to the passages in the paper body that back it, or marked "no supporting evidence found".

Reproducibility

A 15-item reporting checklist with the quote that earned each credit and the items that are missing.

Evidence gaps

Rule-based flags for a reviewer, such as a figure in the abstract that never appears in the results.

Report: HTML Markdown JSON
Ratings and scores are lexical and numeric heuristics over what the paper itself states. They are aids for a human reviewer, not verdicts: check every quote against the cited page.
